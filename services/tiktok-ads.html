Overview

Why It Works for Escape Rooms

Escape rooms are made for TikTok: suspense, puzzles, reactions and a ticking clock. We turn that energy into native video that feels like content, not ads — and reaches the Gen Z and millennial groups who book together.

From creator collaborations to Spark Ads that boost your best organic posts, we build reach that converts into bookings.

Clues & Answers

Frequently Asked

Is TikTok right for my venue?

If your players are 18–34 or you run groups and parties, TikTok is one of the most efficient ways to build awareness.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. We work with creators and players, and can produce content with minimal time from you.

What are Spark Ads?

Spark Ads let us promote real organic posts — yours or a creator’s — so engagement stays on the original post.
