Why It Works for Escape Rooms

Technical Breakdown

Inside the Mechanism

Exactly what we configure, build and manage — no black boxes.

Tracking foundationMeta Pixel plus the Conversions API (server-side) with event deduplication, so purchases are still reported when browsers block cookies.
Audience strategyCustom audiences from website visitors, booking-page abandoners and past customer lists; lookalikes built from your highest-value bookers; broad Advantage+ testing alongside.
Creative testingA structured matrix of hooks × formats (Reels, Stories, carousels, static) with dynamic creative so winners surface fast and fatigue is caught early.
Video ad productionShort, vertical, story-driven video built for the first three seconds — the countdown, the lock, the reaction.
RetargetingSequenced retargeting for visitors who viewed a room or started a booking, with urgency and social-proof messaging.
Lead generation funnelsInstant-form and landing-page funnels for corporate team building, birthdays and private events, routed straight to your inbox or CRM.
