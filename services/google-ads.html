Overview

Why It Works for Escape Rooms

When someone types “escape room near me”, “team building activities” or “things to do tonight”, they are ready to book. Google Ads puts your rooms in front of those players at the exact moment of intent — above your competitors and above the map pack.

We don’t optimise for clicks. We connect Google Ads to your booking platform so every campaign is judged on the revenue it actually produces, then let smart bidding chase the bookings worth the most.

Technical Breakdown

Inside the Mechanism

Exactly what we configure, build and manage — no black boxes.

Campaign architectureSearch campaigns split by intent theme — brand, “near me”, team building, birthdays, date night and competitor terms — each with dedicated ad groups, copy and landing pages. Performance Max layered on top for Maps, YouTube and Display reach.
Conversion trackingGoogle Tag Manager + GA4 + Google Ads purchase conversions with the real booking value passed dynamically from your booking system (e.g. Resova). Enhanced Conversions enabled to recover attribution lost to cookie restrictions.
Bidding strategyStart on Maximize Conversions to build data, then graduate to value-based bidding (Target ROAS) once conversion volume is stable — so the algorithm prioritises high-value group and corporate bookings.
Local visibilityLocation assets linked to your Google Business Profile, radius targeting around your venue, and ad scheduling weighted towards the days and session slots you most need to fill.
Negative keyword controlWeekly search-term reviews and shared negative lists to block wasted spend on queries like “free”, “jobs”, “DIY”, “printable” and “online escape room”.
ReportingMonthly performance reports tied to bookings and revenue — not vanity metrics — with a live dashboard you can check any time.
